What’s the catch?

Two things, and they’re both on this page. The ad budget is yours, paid straight to Meta on your card, and you pick the amount. And I get 6% of any job my ads bring you. That’s it. If they bring you nothing, I make nothing.

How much do I have to spend on ads?

You decide. At the sit-down we work it out from your own numbers: what a job is worth to you and how many more you can take on. You can raise it, lower it or stop it anytime.

I tried Facebook ads before and got junk leads.

That happens a lot, and it’s usually the form. The default one is so easy that people tap through it without meaning it. I use the version that makes them review their info and answer a couple of questions about the roof. You get fewer leads, but they’re real.

Why Facebook and Instagram instead of Google?

Google is great for people already searching “roofer near me”, but every roofer in your area is bidding on those same people. Facebook and Instagram get in front of the homeowner before they search, while their roof is getting old and they haven’t called anyone yet. It works alongside whatever you already do. You don’t have to give anything up.

What do you need from me?

Photos or videos of your jobs (phone shots are fine), access to your Facebook Page and Instagram account so the ads run under your name, and a card on your ad account for the budget. I handle the building and the day-to-day. If you don’t have a Facebook Page yet, we’ll set one up together.

Do you only work with roofers?

Yes. Working with one trade means the ads, the questions on the form and the follow-up are all built around how roofing actually sells.

Want to see what your ads would look like?

Give me 30 minutes on a video call. I’ll ask about your numbers and show you exactly what the ads would look like for your company. If it doesn’t make sense, no hard feelings.
